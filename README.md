> ## Problem Statement: Neuro-Inclusive Urban Navigator
> For most people, a trip to the local grocery store, a mall, or a public library is a routine task.
However, for individuals who with sensory processing sensitivities—including those with Autism,
ADHD, PTSD, or Sensory Processing Disorder (SPD)—the physical environment can be a
source of intense distress.
A sudden blast of loud music in a cafe, flickering fluorescent lights in a pharmacy, or an
unexpected crowd in a narrow aisle can lead to sensory overload, anxiety, or physical
exhaustion. Currently, standard navigation apps like Google Maps focus on what a place sells or
how popular it is, but they almost entirely ignore the sensory atmosphere of the space.
Your goal is to build a community-driven web platform that allows users to map and filter local
businesses and public spaces based on their sensory profile.
The platform must enable users to contribute real-time sensory reports and allow others to plan
their day by finding environments that match their specific neurological needs (like "Find me a
quiet library with natural lighting").
